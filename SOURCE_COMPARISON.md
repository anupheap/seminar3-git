## Source corrections made for mathematical accuracy

- The second Canva deck's dependence definition is worded incorrectly on page 7. Dependence means a **nonzero** coefficient vector solves $A\mathbf c=0$, or one vector is a combination of the others.
- `Presentation2.pptx` slide 6 describes its rectangular outer-product example in the wrong order. The displayed multiplication is a **column times a row**.
- The second Canva deck's shorthand $N(A)=0$ is written as $N(A)=\{\mathbf0\}$ in the rebuilt deck.

All other numerical examples in the active slides use the supplied source matrices and results. The source notes under each slide point to the relevant file and page or slide.
